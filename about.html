Our Story & Mission

Sharing the heart of Sri Lanka through first-hand travel journalism, local guides, and ethical wildlife encounters.

01. Field Tested

First-Hand Island Routes

Every train timetable, sunrise hike trailhead, and safari gate tip published on Adventure Guide comes from real journeys across Sri Lanka's nine provinces.

02. Community First

Supporting Local Homestays

We spotlight family-run guesthouses, independent tuk-tuk drivers, and certified local naturalists so your travel spend stays within Sri Lankan communities.

03. Ethical Wildlife

Respecting Nature

We only recommend national parks, elephant transit homes, and marine operators that follow strict wildlife distance and conservation guidelines.
