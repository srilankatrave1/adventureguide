Seasonal Planner

Best Time to Visit Sri Lanka

Thanks to two complementary monsoon seasons, Sri Lanka is a year-round tropical destination.

December – April

West & South Coast + Hill Country

Ideal sunshine for Galle, Mirissa, Bentota, Colombo, Yala safaris, and crisp clear mornings in Ella and Nuwara Eliya.

May – September

East Coast & Northern Peninsula

Prime dry weather for surfing in Arugam Bay, snorkelling Pigeon Island in Trincomalee, and exploring Jaffna.

July – August

Cultural Triangle & Kandy Perahera

Witness the grand Kandy Esala Perahera festival and the Great Elephant Gathering at Minneriya National Park.
