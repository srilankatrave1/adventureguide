Best Time to Visit Sri Lanka

Thanks to dual monsoons, Sri Lanka offers wonderful weather somewhere on the island all year round.

December – April

West & South Coast + Hill Country

Ideal sunshine for Galle, Mirissa, Bentota, Colombo, Yala safaris, and crisp mornings in Ella.

May – September

East Coast & Northern Peninsula

Prime dry weather for surfing in Arugam Bay and snorkelling Pigeon Island in Trincomalee.

July – August

Cultural Triangle & Festivals

Witness the grand Kandy Esala Perahera and the Elephant Gathering at Minneriya National Park.
