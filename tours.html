Sri Lanka Tour Packages for Every Kind of Traveller

A Sri Lanka tour is one of the few trips where you can climb an ancient rock fortress, ride a train through tea country, spot leopards at dawn and end the week on a beach, all without long transfers. Our Sri Lanka tour packages are built around that variety. Every itinerary includes a private driver-guide, hand-picked stays and a pace that leaves time to enjoy each stop. Why choose a Sri Lanka travel package

The island is small on the map but varied on the ground. Roads in the hills are slow and winding, and distances that look short can take half a day. A well-planned Sri Lanka travel package removes that guesswork. Your driver-guide handles the route, hotels are confirmed before you land, and the order of stops follows the geography rather than forcing you to backtrack. You spend your time at the temple, the park or the beach instead of sorting out logistics.

Sri Lanka holiday packages by theme

Our Sri Lanka holiday packages are grouped into five themes. Honeymoon trips pair scenic train rides and private dinners with quiet beach stays. Family itineraries keep days short and include an elephant orphanage, village safaris and resorts with pools. Wildlife trips head to Yala, Wilpattu and Minneriya for jeep safaris, with whale watching off Mirissa as an optional extra. Beach holidays combine a few days in the hills with a relaxed finish on the southern coast. Heritage routes cover Sigiriya, Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa and Kandy, including several UNESCO sites.

Planning a Sri Lanka tour from Australia

Many of our guests start in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane or Perth. Flights to Colombo usually involve one connection, so allow about a day for the journey and plan a restful first night. The Australian summer school holidays and Christmas break fall in the dry season for the west and south coasts, which makes December to March a popular window for a beach-focused trip. Wildlife travellers often prefer February to July for Yala. Entry rules for Australian passport holders can change, so confirm the current visa requirements before booking flights.

What Sri Lanka tour packages from Australia include

If you are comparing Sri Lanka tour packages from Australia, check what is actually covered. Ours include private air-conditioned transport, a licensed local driver-guide, accommodation, daily breakfast, entrance fees for the main sites listed in each itinerary and airport transfers on arrival and departure. International flights are not included, which lets you use your preferred airline, frequent flyer points or a stopover in Singapore or Kuala Lumpur. We can suggest arrival times that suit your flight so the first day does not feel rushed.
